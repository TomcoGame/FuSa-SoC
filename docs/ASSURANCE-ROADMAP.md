## Candidate standards

IEC 61508 for general functional safety; ISO 26262 for automotive applications; ISO/SAE 21434 for automotive cybersecurity. Applicability and integrity targets depend on the defined use case. Compliance requires substantially more than completing this checklist.

## Evidence requirements

Each claim must identify the source revision, configuration, assumptions, fault model, test or analysis method, result, reviewer and outstanding limitations. Track negative test results and unresolved issues as first-class evidence.
